## 試しの決まり(公開の前に決めた)
- 問い: 型紙の作り手が、計算の下見に1件15ドル払うか
- 合格の線: 3件の出品の公開から30日で、有料の注文が合わせて3件以上 → 続ける。0〜2件 → 理由(見られた回数・問い合わせの数)を書いて、続けるかをオーナーが決める

## 出品1 編み物
- Title: I will check the stitch math in your knitting pattern for every size
- Category: Writing & Translation > Proofreading & Editing(出品の画面で近いものを選ぶ)
- Tags: knitting pattern, tech edit, pattern grading, stitch count, knitwear design
- Price: Basic $15 / 1 pattern, all sizes / 2 days delivery / 1 revision(注文は昼のセッションで GM が処理するので1日でなく2日にした)
- 分類の校正では「Grammar & spelling」が外せないため、出品文に「typos I spot along the way」を入れ、「full copy edit ではない」と書いた
- Description:
  Before you pay for a full tech edit or send your pattern to test knitters, get the numbers checked.
  I go through your knitting pattern row by row and recalculate every stitch count, increase, decrease and repeat, for every size. You get a short report that lists each line where the numbers don't add up, with the calculation shown, so you can fix it in minutes.
  What I check: stitch counts after increases/decreases, repeat math, totals per size, numbers in charts vs. written rows (when both are given), gauge-based measurements in the schematic.
  What I don't do: style-sheet editing, wording, layout, or test knitting.
  How it works: I use my own checking program and AI tools to recompute the numbers, then review every flagged line myself before delivery. If you prefer no AI involvement, please don't order this gig.
  Send me: the pattern (PDF, Word or text), the size list, and your gauge.
